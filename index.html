ABOUT US

Creating change through collective action.

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non-profit organization founded on September 23, 2020. Based in Chhattisgarh, the foundation works towards community development and social empowerment through meaningful and sustainable initiatives.

Its work focuses on education, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental conservation, community support and skill development.
